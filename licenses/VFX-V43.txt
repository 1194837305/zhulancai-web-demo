# V4.3 Shader references and provenance

- Waterfront: wave/refraction/absorption architecture inspired by UnionBytes, Achim Menzel (AiYori), copyright 2019. https://godotshaders.com/shader/realistic-water/ — MIT. This project implements its own compact Godot4/orthographic/Compatibility adaptation; no third-party textures or caustic arrays are downloaded.
- Weather: layered parallax/explicit-clock approach from Brian Smith (Steampunkdemon). https://godotshaders.com/shader/rain-and-snow-with-parallax-scrolling-effect/ — MIT. Project-authored circular-cell snow and fine line rain; no other assets used.
- Hurt: compact adaptation of ratko's CC0 noise-edged vignette. https://godotshaders.com/shader/damage-hurt-pulsing-blood-red-screen-edge/ — CC0. Palette, strength, clock and pulse behavior changed for accessibility and pastel UI.
- Attack research: Ali, Procedural Idyllic Slash / Spellbook (copyright 2025, MIT), https://godotshaders.com/shader/procedural-idyllic-slash-2d/ and https://github.com/Alkaliii/spellbook-gd . Studied procedural sweep/progress control; did not vendor its >100 uniforms. skill_ribbon and skill_impact are independent project-authored shaders.
- Godot4 depth conversion reference: https://docs.godotengine.org/en/stable/tutorials/shaders/advanced_postprocessing.html ; explicit OpenGL/Vulkan NDC conversion maintained.
